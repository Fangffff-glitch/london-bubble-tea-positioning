## Source quality

Google reviews capture self-selected accounts. Platform moderation, incentives, translations, visitors versus regulars, store age, menu breadth and neighbourhood context can affect the corpus. Names and nationality cannot establish actual demographics; self-descriptions are not independently verified.

This release analyses the complete accessible snapshot of **these listings**, not all London stores or other platforms. It does not supplement with TripAdvisor or delivery reviews, and does not present stars from the same Google records as independent triangulation.

## What should be collected next

Matched peak-hour queue observations; order-to-handover timestamps; channel demand; staff hours; menu mix; waste/remakes; variable costs and rent/volume constraints. Current franchise footprint and neighbourhood competitor density require separate verification before being used as explanatory variables. These inputs have not been fabricated or inferred from Google review totals.
