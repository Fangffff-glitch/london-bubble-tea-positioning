## Sensitive and ambiguous content

T4-0214 contains an unverified reviewer claim about a review incentive during a remake. This is a reason to consider incentive/selection bias, not evidence that a store systematically manipulates reviews. No staff identity is republished. Other health, conduct or discrimination allegations remain unverified customer accounts; they are not promoted as factual brand claims.

T4-1320 is an ambiguous emoji-only text; TRUEDAN-0349 has unclear phrasing; TRUEDAN-0369 is an implausible joke. These receive `unclear`, not a forced positive/negative interpretation. Brief generic praise is still positive. TRUEDAN-0417 is anticipatory rather than a report of consumption and is neutral.

## Outstanding human work

No independent human validation or inter-rater agreement has been completed. The [author worksheet](author_review.csv) is intentionally blank in its human fields. It contains up to ten records per available store/label stratum, selected by capture order. Check original wording, translation, sentiment and theme omissions; retain disagreement notes. This targeted worksheet supports correction, not an unbiased accuracy estimate.

Source IDs and hashes aid traceability but Google does not guarantee that an ID can be opened directly. Public listing links, displayed ages and the private raw archive are the available retrieval aids. Public numerical reproducibility is stronger than independently repeatable access to historical source text.
